Trade finder

Tick the players you would give up, the opponent players you want, and the categories you need. The finder tries every combination and lists the trades that add the most in your needed categories while being at least as good for you as for the opponent. Player value is a z-score: how far each stat sits above or below the league’s rotation players. FG% and FT% are measured as makes above expectation, so shooting volume counts.

Rules

Value for each team is the sum of its category changes, with needed categories counted at the weight above. A trade must be worth at least as much to you as to the opponent, and the opponent’s gain must meet the minimum (0 means they cannot lose value; a negative number lets them lose a little).
